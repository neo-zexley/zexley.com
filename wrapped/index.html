How to export a WhatsApp chat

  1. Open the group chat in WhatsApp.
  2. iPhone: tap the group name → Export Chat → Without Media. Android: ⋮ menu → More → Export chat → Without media.
  3. Save the file to your phone or computer (Files app, Drive, or email it to yourself).
  4. Drop the .zip or .txt here. A zip is opened right here in your browser. If your browser can’t, unzip it and drop the _chat.txt inside.

How to export a Telegram chat

  1. Open the group chat in the Telegram Desktop app on a computer.
  2. Click the ⋮ menu at the top right → Export chat history.
  3. Untick photos, videos and other media (they aren’t needed) and set Format to Machine-readable JSON. Then click Export.
  4. Drop the result.json from the export folder here (or a .zip of that folder). The HTML format won’t work.

How to export an Instagram or Messenger chat

  1. In Instagram, or in Facebook or Messenger, open Accounts Center and find the option to download your information.
  2. Choose to download it to your device. Select only your messages if you can, and set the format to JSON (HTML won’t work). Pick a date range that covers 2026.
  3. Send the request. Meta prepares the file and tells you when it’s ready, which can take a while.
  4. Download the .zip and drop it here. If it holds several chats, you pick one. Or drop the message_1.json (plus message_2.json and so on for long chats) from that chat’s folder.

Menu names change from time to time, so follow Meta’s own help pages if yours look different. The zip is opened only in this tab, and only its chat files are read. Times are shown in this device’s time zone.

Discord: a recap of your own year

Good to know first: Discord’s data package contains only messages you sent, not anyone else’s. So for Discord you get a personal recap: your top channels and DMs, your busiest day and hour, your words, emoji, streaks and personal badges. It can’t show who talked most in a server.

  1. In Discord, open User Settings → Data & Privacy and find the option to request your data. Make sure Messages is selected.
  2. Discord emails you a download link when the package is ready. Discord says this can take up to 30 days.
  3. Download the .zip and drop it here as it is (no need to unzip). Then pick all channels for your whole year, or just one. You can also drop a single messages.json (or an older messages.csv) from its Messages folder.

Menus change from time to time, so follow Discord’s own help page about the data package if yours look different. The zip is opened only in this tab, and only its message files are read. Your account details, activity and payment files in the package are never opened. Times are shown in this device’s time zone.

No. The file is read by JavaScript in this tab and never sent anywhere — not to us, not to anyone. There is no server step. You can even turn on airplane mode after the page loads and it still works.

Discord’s data package contains only the messages you sent, not anyone else’s. So the Discord recap is about you: your channels, busiest days and hours, words, emoji and personal badges. It never shows other people’s messages, because it never has them.

Yes. WhatsApp exports in German, Spanish, Portuguese, French, Italian and Dutch are read, including their date formats and “media omitted” lines. Other languages often work too, but some counts (like photos) may be off.
