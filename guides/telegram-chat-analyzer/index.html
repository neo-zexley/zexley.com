How to analyze a Telegram group chat (stats, who texts most, busiest times) privately

Last updated November 23, 2026.

To find out who sends the most messages in a Telegram group, when the group is busiest, or which emoji you use most, it helps to have a copy of the chat. Telegram Desktop's Export chat history tool gives you that copy as a machine-readable JSON file, and a Telegram chat analyzer can then count it. This guide covers what you can learn from a Telegram export, how to get the stats on your device without uploading the chat, how to count it by hand for free, and the limits to expect.

What stats you can get from a Telegram export

Telegram's data export schema says each message in the JSON export has a date, the sender's name, and its text, and that regular messages are marked separately from service messages such as joins and pins. From that, Group Chat Wrapped computes the following Telegram group chat statistics. The first four are free.

  • Total messages for 2026 (or for the whole file if it has no 2026 messages), plus how many people took part and how many days had at least one message. Free.
  • Busiest times: the busiest day (with its message count), the busiest hour, the busiest weekday, and about how many messages a day the group sends. Free.
  • Who texts the most: the top talker's message count and share of the chat, plus a bar showing each member's share for up to 8 members. Free.
  • Longest streak: the most days in a row with at least one message, with the start and end dates. Free.
  • Emoji of the year: the group's top emoji and how often it was used, plus each member's favorite emoji (up to 8 members). Unlock.
  • Word of the year: the most-used word and four runners-up. Common words like “the” and members' own names are skipped. Unlock.
  • Laughs and media: how many laughs (haha, lol, 😂, 🤣) and how many photos, videos, and stickers were shared. Photos, stickers, voice messages, and files are counted from the message entries in result.json, so you can leave media off when you export. Unlock.
  • Up to 16 kind awards picked by simple rules, such as Night Owl (most messages between midnight and 4 AM), Early Bird (5 to 8 AM), Conversation Starter (breaking the silence after 6 or more quiet hours), and Speedy Replier (typical reply time). Awards are for fun, not verdicts about anyone. Unlock.
  • A summary slide and the full share image, made on your device. Unlock.

The recap never shows the text of anyone's messages. It shows names, counts, top words and emoji, and awards.

How to analyze a Telegram group chat, step by step

  1. Install Telegram Desktop and sign in Telegram's export tool is in the official Telegram Desktop app on a computer (Windows, macOS, or Linux), not in the iPhone or Android apps. Install the latest version and sign in.
  2. Open the group and choose Export chat history Open the group in Telegram Desktop, click the three-dot menu at the top right, and choose Export chat history.
  3. Leave media off and set the format to JSON Untick photos, videos, and other media, because the stats do not need them and the export stays much smaller. Set Format to Machine-readable JSON. The HTML format will not work.
  4. Export and find result.json Click Export and leave Telegram open until it finishes. The export folder usually contains a file named result.json, plus any media folders you included.

Doing it by hand, and what is free

By hand, free. result.json is plain text, so you can count it yourself. According to Telegram's export schema, each message has a "type" (message for regular messages, service for things like joins and pins), a "from" field with the sender's name, and a "date" timestamp such as 2026-09-03T17:05:43. Open the file in a text editor and search for a member's name to get a rough count, keeping in mind that the name also matches wherever it appears in message text. A spreadsheet or a short script on your own computer can tally senders, days, and hours more exactly. Treat a hand count as approximate. If you just want to read the chat, Telegram's HTML export opens in a web browser, but it does not count anything.

Limits to know before you start

  • Only what is in your export. The recap counts only the messages in your result.json. If part of the group's history is not in the file, it is not in the recap.
  • A computer, not a phone. Telegram documents the export tool in Telegram Desktop, so you need a Windows, macOS, or Linux computer to make the file.
  • JSON and one chat at a time. The HTML export is not read, and neither is a full-account export from Export Telegram data. Export just the group you want.
  • Service messages are skipped. Joins, pins, title changes, and calls are not counted as messages. Messages that have no sender name in the export are counted under Deleted Account.
  • 2026 first. If the chat has 2026 messages, the recap counts 2026 only. Otherwise it counts everything and says so.
  • Who talked most is a message count. It is not a measure of who wrote the longest messages. Many short replies add up.
  • Other apps. WhatsApp, Instagram, and Messenger exports also work, in the formats described in our guides. A Discord data package gives a personal recap of your own messages only. iMessage and Signal are not supported yet.

Common questions

Is there a free way to analyze a Telegram chat?

Yes. You can count result.json by hand in a text editor, or with a spreadsheet or short script on your own computer, for free. In Group Chat Wrapped, the sample chat and the first 4 slides of your own chat are free: total messages, busiest day and hour, who talked most, and your longest streak. A one-time $4.99 unlocks the rest.

Can I see how many messages each person sent in a Telegram group?

Yes, from a JSON export. Group Chat Wrapped shows the top talker's message count and each member's share of the chat as a bar for up to 8 members, in the free slides.

Is my Telegram chat uploaded or read by AI?

No. The file is read by code in your browser tab and is never sent to us or anyone else. No AI reads your chat: the stats come from plain code. The tool and this page were made with AI assistance.

Can I export a Telegram chat on my phone?

Telegram documents the export tool in Telegram Desktop on a computer. We do not have official phone-app steps for a JSON chat export, so use Desktop, then drop the file into Group Chat Wrapped in a browser on that computer.

Why does it say my file is a full account export?

Group Chat Wrapped reads one chat at a time. A full-account export, made from Settings, then Advanced, then Export Telegram data, is not read. Open the group in Telegram Desktop, choose Export chat history, and pick Machine-readable JSON instead.

Can I use Telegram's HTML export?

No. Group Chat Wrapped does not read the HTML export (messages.html or export_results.html). Export the chat again with Format set to Machine-readable JSON and drop result.json.

Why do some numbers look off?

The recap can only count what is in your file, and counts can be imperfect. Service messages, such as joins, pins, title changes, and calls, are skipped. Messages that have no sender name in the export are counted under Deleted Account.
