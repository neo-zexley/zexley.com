Privacy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Zexley is an assumed business name of Bloom Commerce LLC, an Indiana limited liability company. This page describes what happens to information on this site today, and when you buy from or email us. If that changes, we will update this page first.

This site does not track you

This site uses no analytics, ads, or cookies. We do not have accounts or a login. We do not run third-party advertising or social pixels.

Hosting

Our web host (GitHub Pages) may keep standard technical logs of page requests, such as IP address and the page address, for security. We do not receive or use these logs.

Free tools run in your browser

The rental listing red-flag checker runs entirely in your browser. What you type there — including the listing details and your Yes/No answers — is not sent to us or anyone else, and is not stored after you close the page. We never see the listing you are checking.

If a future tool will send or save any of your information, we will say so on that tool and in this policy before you use it. We will not quietly start collecting answers from a tool that is described as running only in your browser.

Group Chat Wrapped 2026

Group Chat Wrapped 2026 reads the WhatsApp or Telegram chat export you choose inside your browser. A Telegram export can also contain account IDs and other details; the page ignores them and never saves them. The chat, including other people's names and messages, is never sent to us or anyone else and is never stored on our servers. Closing the tab or pressing "Start over" clears it from memory. The share image is created on your device and is only shared if you share it.

What is stored on your device, and only if you start an unlock: just before you go to checkout, your browser saves the finished stats for that recap in its local storage on your device. Those stats are the group name, members' display names as they appear in the chat, message counts and shares, busiest day/hour/weekday, streak, the top five words and emoji, the award titles, winners and one-line descriptions, a random reference code, and timestamps. No message text is saved. This lets your recap reopen, unlocked, when Stripe sends you back. The saved stats are deleted automatically 2 hours after you leave for checkout if you don't complete payment, 24 hours after you return from a completed payment, or immediately when you press "Forget my recap now" (or clear your browser's site data). If you pay but the saved stats have already expired, we keep only an "unlocked" marker with no chat data, for 24 hours. This data stays in your browser and is never sent to us, to Stripe, or to anyone else. We do not use cookies or analytics on this page.

Email

If you write to neo@zexley.com, we use your email address and the contents of your message to reply. If you ask to join an early list (for example, for the paid rental-check report), we also use that address to email you when that product opens. We do not add you to an unrelated marketing list, and we do not share your address for others to market to you.

You can ask us to remove you from an early list or to delete an email thread by writing to the same address. If you click an email link on this site, your own email app opens, and nothing is sent unless you send it.

If you buy a paid product

If you check out with Stripe, Stripe collects your payment details and the information you enter there. Stripe processes the card. We receive payment confirmation and the intake fields, not your full card number.

For a Group Chat Wrapped 2026 unlock, Stripe collects your payment details and email. We receive the payment confirmation, your email, the amount, and a random reference code created by your browser, never your chat or its stats.

For the 48-Hour HVAC Visibility Pack, the information you enter at checkout may include email, business name, website or Google Business Profile URL, and service area.

If you reply to our intake email or write to us about an order, we keep that correspondence so we can deliver the product and answer you.

How we use information, and for how long

We use the information above to reply to you, to take payment and deliver a product you bought, to send receipts and follow-up about that order, and to keep a business record. We do not ask for Google, website, or email passwords. We do not sell your information.

We keep order and delivery records as needed for accounting and to support work we already did. Early-list signups are kept until we have emailed you that the product is open, or until you ask us to remove you, whichever comes first — and we may keep a brief record of the request.

If we collect more later

We do not currently have user accounts, saved tool history, or mailing lists beyond people who emailed us or asked to join an early list. If we start collecting new kinds of information, we will update this page and tell you what is collected and why before you use that feature.

Your requests

You can email neo@zexley.com to ask what we have on file, to correct it, or to ask us to delete an early-list signup or email thread. We will need enough information to find your request. For Group Chat Wrapped 2026, we never receive your chat or its stats, so we cannot delete those from our systems — they are only on your device. You can clear them yourself with "Forget my recap now" or by clearing this site's data in your browser.
