Privacy

Zexley is a brand of Bloom Commerce LLC (Indiana). This is a short, plain-language note about information we collect when you buy or email us about the HVAC Visibility Pack.

What we collect

If you check out with Stripe, Stripe collects your payment details and the information you enter there: email, business name, website or Google Business Profile URL, and service area. Stripe processes the card. We receive payment confirmation and the intake fields, not your full card number.

If you reply to our intake email or write to us, we keep the contents of that correspondence so we can deliver the pack and answer you.

How we use it

We use this information to take payment, deliver the pack, send receipts and follow-up, and keep a business record. We do not ask for Google, website, or email passwords. We do not sell your information. We do not use it to send unrelated marketing lists.

How long we keep it

We keep order and delivery records as needed for accounting and to support the work we already did. You can email us to ask what we have on file or to correct it.
