How to download your Facebook Messenger chat history

Last updated November 22, 2026. Steps checked against Meta's official Messenger help the same day.

Messenger chats are exported from Meta's Export your information tool, not from a button inside a single thread. You choose Messages, pick JSON, wait for Meta to prepare a ZIP, then download it. This page follows Meta's official help and then explains how to use the file in a private recap.

Export Messenger messages

These steps follow Export a copy of your Facebook information in the Messenger Help Center. You can also reach the same tool from Facebook or Messenger settings.

  1. Open Meta Account settings On a computer, Meta's help says: click your profile picture, then Settings and privacy, then Settings, then Meta Account, then Your information and permissions. In the Facebook or Messenger app, look in Settings for Accounts Center or Meta Account. Meta says it is gradually updating people, so you may see either label.
  2. Create an export to your device Open Export your information, then Create export. Select the profile you want, then choose Export to device.
  3. Select messages, a date range, and JSON Choose specific information if you can, and include Messages. Pick a date range that covers the year you care about. Set the format to JSON. Meta describes JSON as a machine-readable format, and HTML as an easy-to-view format. Group Chat Wrapped only reads JSON.
  4. Start the export Click or tap Start export. Meta says you will be asked for your password, and may be asked for extra verification. Keep the request private.
  5. Download it within four days When the export is ready, Meta says you will get an email and a Facebook notification, and that you have 4 days to download it from Available downloads. Save the ZIP somewhere only you can open.

Meta says a deactivated profile or one scheduled for deletion cannot be exported until you reactivate it or cancel the deletion. It also reminds you that the download can contain private information, so store it carefully and be cautious if you upload it to another service. Group Chat Wrapped does not ask you to upload it: the ZIP is read in your browser.

Find a chat in the ZIP

Look for message_1.json files. Long conversations may continue in message_2.json and further numbered files in the same folder. Folder names have changed over time, so search the archive rather than relying on one path. If several chats are present, keep the whole ZIP private and pick one chat later on your device.

Common questions

How long does it take?

Meta does not promise a short turnaround on the Messenger export page we checked. Instagram's related export help says it may take up to 30 days. Plan ahead if you want a recap for a launch or a holiday share.

What about end-to-end encrypted chats?

Meta documents a separate download for end-to-end encrypted Messenger chats, and says it is currently only available on a computer: open facebook.com, click Messenger, then Options, Chat history, Last backup, then Download chat backup. Group Chat Wrapped is built for the Accounts Center JSON export (message_1.json files). We have not tested the separate encrypted-chat backup format, so we do not claim it will open.

Can I export just one thread from inside the chat?

The official path we are following is an account export from Export your information, with Messages selected. That ZIP can contain many chats. In Group Chat Wrapped you pick one chat on your device.

The ZIP is too large for my phone.

Request Messages only, tighten the date range, and choose lower media quality. If the ZIP still fails on a phone, unzip it on a computer and drop that chat's message_1.json plus any numbered follow-on files into the recap. Very large Meta downloads may fail in a phone browser.
