How to analyze a WhatsApp group chat (stats, who texts most, busiest times) privately

Last updated November 22, 2026.

WhatsApp's official help does not describe a built-in way to see who sends the most messages in a group, when the group is busiest, or which emoji you use most. To find out, you need a copy of the chat. WhatsApp's own Export chat feature gives you that copy as a text file, and a WhatsApp chat analyzer can then count it. This guide covers what you can learn from an official export, how to get the stats on your device without uploading the chat, how to count it by hand for free, and the limits to expect.

What stats you can get from a WhatsApp export

The export lists each message with a date, time, and sender name. From that, Group Chat Wrapped counts the following. The first four are free.

  • Total messages for 2026 (or for the whole file if it has no 2026 messages), plus how many people took part and how many days had at least one message. Free.
  • Busiest times: the busiest day (with its message count), the busiest hour, the busiest weekday, and about how many messages a day the group sends. Free.
  • Who texts the most: the top talker's message count and share of the chat, plus a bar showing each member's share for up to 8 members. Free.
  • Longest streak: the most days in a row with at least one message, with the start and end dates. Free.
  • Emoji of the year: the group's top emoji and how often it was used, plus each member's favorite emoji (up to 8 members). Unlock.
  • Word of the year: the most-used word and four runners-up. Common words like “the” and members' own names are skipped. Unlock.
  • Laughs and media: how many laughs (haha, lol, 😂, 🤣) and how many photos, videos, and stickers were shared. Media is counted from the placeholder lines the export leaves behind, so you can export without media. Unlock.
  • Up to 16 kind awards picked by simple rules, such as Night Owl (most messages between midnight and 4 AM), Early Bird (5 to 8 AM), Conversation Starter (breaking the silence after 6 or more quiet hours), and Speedy Replier (typical reply time). Awards are for fun, not verdicts about anyone. Unlock.
  • A summary slide and the full share image, made on your device. Unlock.

The recap never shows the text of anyone's messages. It shows names, counts, top words and emoji, and awards.

How to analyze a WhatsApp group chat, step by step

  2. Save the file where you can reach it On iPhone you get a ZIP file. On Android you get a .txt file, or a ZIP if that is what you saved. Save it to your phone or computer, for example in Files or Drive, or email it to yourself.

Doing it by hand, and what is free

By hand, free. A WhatsApp export is plain text, so you can count it yourself. Open the .txt file (unzip it first on iPhone) in a text editor and search for a member's name, or import it into a spreadsheet, split out the sender name on each line, and count the lines per sender. Messages that run over several lines, system notices, and date formats that differ by phone and language make this fiddly, so treat a hand count as approximate. Using software on your own device keeps the file off other people's servers.

Limits to know before you start

  • Only what is in your export. History often starts around the day you joined or the last time you restored a backup. WhatsApp says view-once messages are not included in the iPhone export.
  • Phones, not Windows. WhatsApp's export help is written for iPhone and Android. When we checked the Windows help page on November 22, 2026, WhatsApp said the feature was not available on that platform.
  • 2026 first. If the chat has 2026 messages, the recap counts 2026 only. Otherwise it counts everything and says so.
  • Counts can be imperfect. Exports vary by phone, language, and settings. WhatsApp exports in German, Spanish, Portuguese, French, Italian, and Dutch are read, as well as English. Other languages often work too, but some counts (like photos) may be off.
  • Who talked most is a message count. It is not a measure of who wrote the longest messages. Many short replies add up.
  • Other apps. Instagram, Messenger, and Telegram exports also work, in the formats described in our guides. A Discord data package gives a personal recap of your own messages only. iMessage and Signal are not supported yet.
