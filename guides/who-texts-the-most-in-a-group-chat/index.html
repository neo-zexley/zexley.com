How to see who texts the most in a group chat

Last updated November 22, 2026.

Most group chats do not show a simple scoreboard. WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, and Telegram will show recent media, group settings, or member lists, but they do not give you a reliable official tally of who sent the most messages this year. To get that number, you export a copy of the chat and count it.

You can count the file yourself in a spreadsheet. Or you can drop it into a recap tool. If you use Group Chat Wrapped, the counting happens on your device. The chat is never uploaded, and no AI reads the messages. Group Chat Wrapped 2026 is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Meta, WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, Telegram, or Spotify.

What “texts the most” actually means

In Group Chat Wrapped, “who talked most” is the member who sent the most messages in the period that was counted. That is a message count, not a measure of who wrote the longest notes or who spent the most time in the chat. A person who sends many short replies can outrank someone who writes fewer, longer messages.

The count can only include what is in your export. If you joined mid-year, or restored a phone and lost older history, the numbers will start where your file starts. For WhatsApp, view-once messages are not exported on iPhone, and a group with Restricted chat cannot be exported at all.

How to get the count

  1. Export the group chat Export the chat from the app you use. For WhatsApp, use Export chat and choose Without media. For Telegram, export the chat from Telegram Desktop as machine-readable JSON. For Instagram or Messenger, request an information download from Accounts Center, select Messages, and choose JSON.
  2. Open the file on your device Open Group Chat Wrapped in your browser and drop in the export (a WhatsApp .txt or .zip, a Telegram result.json, or an Instagram or Messenger .zip or message_1.json). The file is read in that tab and is not uploaded.
  3. Read who talked most The free slides include who talked most, along with total messages, the busiest day and hour, and the longest streak. If the export contains 2026 messages, the recap counts 2026 only; otherwise it counts everything in the file and says so.

Use the number kindly

A message count is not a verdict on anyone. Our terms ask you not to use the recap to mock, embarrass, or harass a member of the chat, and not to use a chat you are not part of. If you share a slide, you are sharing people's names and how often they wrote. Ask first.

Common questions

Does WhatsApp have a built-in leaderboard?

No. WhatsApp's official group and export help does not describe a per-member message count or year-in-review. You need a copy of the chat, then a tool or a spreadsheet to count the lines.

Is the person with the most messages the one who talks the most?

It is a count of messages in the export, not of minutes spoken or of characters. Short replies add up. System lines and media placeholders are handled as well as the file allows, and counts can be imperfect when formats or languages vary.

Will the whole group see that I checked?

You are using a file already on your device. Group Chat Wrapped does not post anything for you. If you share a recap image, other people will see names and counts, so ask the group before you post it.

Does this work for iMessage or Signal?

Not yet. It works with WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, and Telegram exports in the formats described in our guides.
