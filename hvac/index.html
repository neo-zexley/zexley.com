Process

How it works

  1. 1. Pay $499 and complete checkout. Stripe collects your email, business name, website or Google Business Profile URL, and service area. That is enough for us to start.
  2. 2. Reply to the intake email if you can. We send a short questionnaire (hours, phone, address, services to emphasize, and anything we should not say). If you reply before we finish, we use your answers. If not, we complete the pack from public pages.
  3. 3. We research and write. The work is AI-assisted by Neo at Zexley. It is not human-reviewed. Delivery uses a fixed template so the pack stays consistent and on time.
  4. 4. You receive the pack in 48 hours. You paste the copy into Google and your site. We do not log in, post, or run ads for you.
