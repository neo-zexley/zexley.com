How to analyze an Instagram group chat (stats, who texts most, busiest times) privately

Last updated November 23, 2026.

To find out who sends the most messages in an Instagram group chat, when the group is busiest, or which emoji you use most, it helps to have a copy of the chat. Instagram does not have an Export chat button inside a thread, so you request a copy of your information from Accounts Center in JSON format, and an Instagram chat analyzer can then count the chat's message files. This guide covers what you can learn from an Instagram download, how to get the stats on your device without uploading the chat, how to count it by hand for free, and the limits to expect.

We make Group Chat Wrapped 2026, the tool described here, so every stat below is one it actually computes. Group Chat Wrapped 2026 is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Meta, WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, Telegram, Discord, or Spotify.

What stats you can get from an Instagram download

In a JSON download, each chat has its own folder with a file named message_1.json, and long chats continue in message_2.json, message_3.json, and so on. In the files Group Chat Wrapped reads, each message has the sender's name, a time, and its text or attachments. From that, Group Chat Wrapped computes the following Instagram group chat statistics. The first four are free.

  • Total messages for 2026 (or for the whole file if it has no 2026 messages), plus how many people took part and how many days had at least one message. Free.
  • Busiest times: the busiest day (with its message count), the busiest hour, the busiest weekday, and about how many messages a day the group sends. Times are shown in your device's time zone. Free.
  • Who texts the most: the top talker's message count and share of the chat, plus a bar showing each member's share for up to 8 members. Free.
  • Longest streak: the most days in a row with at least one message, with the start and end dates. Free.
  • Emoji of the year: the group's top emoji and how often it was used, plus each member's favorite emoji (up to 8 members). Meta's JSON stores emoji and accented letters in a garbled form, and Group Chat Wrapped turns them back into real emoji and letters before counting. Unlock.
  • Word of the year: the most-used word and four runners-up. Common words like “the” and members' own names are skipped. Unlock.
  • Laughs and media: how many laughs (haha, lol, 😂, 🤣) and how many photos, videos, and stickers were shared. Photos, videos, GIFs, voice messages, files, and stickers are counted from the message entries in the JSON files. Unlock.
  • Up to 16 kind awards picked by simple rules, such as Night Owl (most messages between midnight and 4 AM), Early Bird (5 to 8 AM), Conversation Starter (breaking the silence after 6 or more quiet hours), and Speedy Replier (typical reply time). Awards are for fun, not verdicts about anyone. Unlock.
  • A summary slide and the full share image, made on your device. Unlock.

The recap never shows the text of anyone's messages. It shows names, counts, top words and emoji, and awards.

How to analyze an Instagram group chat, step by step

The download steps follow Instagram's help page on reviewing and exporting a copy of your information. Wording can differ slightly between the app and a computer.

  1. Open Accounts Center In Instagram, open Settings, then open Meta Account or Accounts Center, then Your information and permissions. Labels may say Accounts Center or Meta Account. Instagram does not have an Export chat button inside a thread.
  2. Start an export to your device Open Export your information, then Create export. Select your Instagram profile, continue, and choose Export to device.
  3. Choose Messages, a date range, and JSON Choose specific types of information and select Messages if you only need your chats. Pick a date range that covers 2026. Set the format to JSON. HTML will not work in Group Chat Wrapped. Lower media quality keeps the file smaller; the recap does not need photos or videos.
  4. Start the export and wait Click or tap Start export. You may be asked for your password. Instagram says it may take up to 30 days, and that you will get an email and an Instagram notification when it is ready.
  5. Download the ZIP within 4 days When it is ready, download it from Available downloads in the Export your information tool. Instagram says you have 4 days to do that. Save the ZIP somewhere only you can open.

  7. Read the free stats The first 4 slides are free: total messages, busiest day and hour, who talked most, and your longest streak. If the export contains 2026 messages, the recap counts 2026 only; otherwise it counts everything in the file and says so.

Doing it by hand, and what is free

By hand, free. The chat's message_1.json is plain text, so you can count it yourself. In the files Group Chat Wrapped reads, each message has a "sender_name" field with the sender's name and a "timestamp_ms" field with the time in milliseconds, and messages are listed newest first. Open the file in a text editor and search for a member's name to get a rough count, keeping in mind that the name also matches wherever it appears in message text, then repeat in message_2.json and any later files. A spreadsheet or a short script on your own computer can tally senders, days, and hours more exactly. Emoji and accented letters are stored as escaped codes, so searching for an emoji usually finds nothing. Treat a hand count as approximate.

Limits to know before you start

  • Only what is in your download. The recap counts only the messages in the chat's JSON files. If your date range or the download leaves part of the chat out, it is not in the recap.
  • It can take a while. Instagram says the export may take up to 30 days, and that you have 4 days to download it once it is ready.
  • JSON and one chat at a time. The HTML format is not read. If the download holds several chats, you pick one.
  • Big downloads on a phone. Very large Meta downloads may fail in a phone browser. Choose Messages only and lower media quality, or unzip on a computer and drop just that chat's message_1.json (plus any numbered follow-on files).
  • Group notices are recognized in English. Notices Meta writes as ordinary messages, such as naming the group or changing nicknames, are skipped when they are in English. In other languages they may be counted as messages.
  • 2026 first. If the chat has 2026 messages, the recap counts 2026 only. Otherwise it counts everything and says so.
  • Who talked most is a message count. It is not a measure of who wrote the longest messages. Many short replies add up.
  • Other apps. Messenger chats from a Meta download work the same way, and WhatsApp and Telegram exports also work, in the formats described in our guides. A Discord data package gives a personal recap of your own messages only. iMessage and Signal are not supported yet.

Common questions

Is there a free way to analyze an Instagram chat?

Yes. You can count the chat's message_1.json file by hand in a text editor, or with a spreadsheet or short script on your own computer, for free. In Group Chat Wrapped, the sample chat and the first 4 slides of your own chat are free: total messages, busiest day and hour, who talked most, and your longest streak. Can I see how many messages each person sent in an Instagram group chat?

Yes, from a JSON download. Group Chat Wrapped shows the top talker's message count and each member's share of the chat as a bar for up to 8 members, in the free slides.

Is my Instagram chat uploaded or read by AI?

No. The file is read by code in your browser tab and is never sent to us or anyone else. No AI reads your chat: the stats come from plain code. The tool and this page were made with AI assistance.

Is there an Export chat button in an Instagram thread?

No. Instagram does not offer a per-chat export button. You request a copy of your information from Accounts Center and include Messages, then pick the chat in Group Chat Wrapped.

My download has lots of chats. Which one is analyzed?

Only the one you pick. Group Chat Wrapped lists the chats in the ZIP, biggest first, on your device, and the list is not saved. It reads only the chat files (message_1.json, message_2.json, and so on) and never sends or saves the rest of the download.

Can I use Instagram's HTML download?

No. Group Chat Wrapped only reads the JSON files (message_1.json, and message_2.json and so on for long chats). Request your information again with the format set to JSON.

Why do some numbers look off?

The recap can only count what is in your file, and counts can be imperfect. Group notices that Meta writes as ordinary messages in English, such as naming the group, nickname changes, people being added or leaving, and calls, are skipped, and so are reaction notices. Notices in other languages may be counted as messages. Deleted messages may not appear in the download at all.
