How to download your Instagram DMs

Last updated November 22, 2026. Steps checked against Instagram's official help the same day.

Instagram does not have an Export chat button inside a thread. You request a copy of your information from Accounts Center, include Messages, and download a ZIP when Meta says it is ready. That can take a while. This page follows Instagram's own help, then shows you what to do with the file.

Export your Instagram messages

These steps follow Instagram's help page on reviewing and exporting a copy of your information. Wording can differ slightly between the app and a computer, and Meta is rolling out labels that say either Accounts Center or Meta Account.

  1. Open Accounts Center In Instagram, open Settings, then open Meta Account or Accounts Center. Official help describes this as More, then Settings, then Meta Account, then Your information and permissions. On a phone, the same path usually starts in Instagram Settings. Labels may say Accounts Center or Meta Account.
  2. Start an export to your device Open Export your information, then Create export. Select your Instagram profile, continue, and choose Export to device.
  3. Choose messages, a date range, and JSON When you customize the export, choose specific types of information and select Messages if you only need your chats. Pick a date range that covers the year you care about (for a 2026 recap, include 2026). Set the format to JSON. HTML will not work in Group Chat Wrapped. Lower media quality keeps the file smaller; the recap does not need photos or videos.
  4. Start the export and wait Click or tap Start export. You may be asked for your password. Instagram says it may take up to 30 days, and that you will get an email and an Instagram notification when it is ready.
  5. Download the ZIP within four days When it is ready, download it from Available downloads in the Export your information tool. Instagram says you have 4 days to do that. Save the ZIP somewhere only you can open.

Instagram also says the process is password-protected, that you need enough storage on the device, and that a deactivated profile or one scheduled for deletion cannot be exported until you reactivate it or cancel the deletion.

Find the chats in the ZIP

Meta has changed the folder layout over time. Look for files named message_1.json. Long chats are often split into message_2.json, message_3.json, and so on in the same folder. A common current layout puts threads under a path like your_instagram_activity/messages/inbox/; older exports may use messages/inbox/. Search the ZIP for message_1.json rather than assuming one path.

The download can include much more than the one chat you want, including other conversations and profile information. Keep the ZIP private. If you use Group Chat Wrapped, the page opens the ZIP only in your browser, reads the chat JSON files, and does not send or save the rest.

Common questions

Is there an Export chat button in a DM thread?

No. Instagram does not offer a per-chat export button. You request a copy of your information from Accounts Center and include Messages.

Why JSON instead of HTML?

Meta offers both. HTML is meant to be opened like a web page. JSON is the machine-readable format. Group Chat Wrapped only reads the JSON files (message_1.json, and message_2.json and so on for long chats).

Will deleted or unsent messages appear?

Instagram's export help says some information you have deleted may be stored temporarily for safety and security, but will not appear when you access or download your information. Do not expect unsent or deleted messages to show up.

The ZIP is huge, or my phone cannot open it.

Choose Messages only, a tighter date range, and lower media quality next time. If a large ZIP fails on your phone, unzip it on a computer and drop just that chat's message_1.json (plus any numbered follow-on files) into Group Chat Wrapped. Very large Meta downloads may fail in a phone browser.
