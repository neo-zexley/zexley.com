How to make a group chat year-in-review for 2026

Last updated November 22, 2026.

A group chat often holds a year's worth of in-jokes, planning, and check-ins, but the apps themselves do not hand you a recap. A year-in-review is just a readable summary of an export you already have the right to make: how many messages the group sent, who talked most, the busiest day, a longest streak, and (if you want them) light awards.

Group Chat Wrapped 2026 does that for WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, and Telegram, and also makes a personal recap of your own Discord messages from Discord’s data package. It runs on your device, with no upload and no AI reading the chat. The first 4 slides are free. Group Chat Wrapped 2026 is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Meta, WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, Telegram, Discord, or Spotify.

What you will need

  • Membership in the group, and the official export from that app.
  • A few minutes for WhatsApp or Telegram. Instagram and Messenger downloads can take longer, because Meta prepares the file after you request it.
  • A phone or computer browser. You do not need an account on zexley.com.

If you want a recap of who talked most and nothing else, the free slides may be enough. The paid unlock is only if you want the rest of the deck and the share image.

Make the recap

  1. Pick one group and export it Choose a group that actually talked this year. Export it from WhatsApp (Without media), from Telegram Desktop as machine-readable JSON, or from Instagram or Messenger by downloading your information in JSON and including Messages.
  2. Drop the file into Group Chat Wrapped Open the tool in your browser and drop in the export. The file stays on your device. The first 4 slides are free: total messages, busiest day and hour, who talked most, and the longest streak.
  3. Unlock the rest only if you want it A one-time $4.99 unlocks the remaining slides, including kind group awards and the share image, with 14-day refunds. It is not a subscription. Payment is handled by Stripe on its own checkout page. You do not send us the chat.
  4. Share only if the group is comfortable The share image is made on your device and can include a QR code that points at zexley.com/wrapped. Names and counts can still be sensitive. Ask the group before you post it to a story or a chat.

Which export to use

  • WhatsApp, iPhone and Android
  • Instagram DMs
  • Messenger
  • Telegram Desktop JSON
  • Discord data package (a personal recap of your own messages)
  • Just the “who talked most” count
  • Group chat award ideas for an awards night

iMessage and Signal are not supported yet. Discord is supported as a personal recap of your own messages from Discord’s data package. WhatsApp chats in German, Spanish, Portuguese, French, Italian, and Dutch are read, including their date formats.

Common questions

Is there an official WhatsApp Wrapped?

No. WhatsApp does not offer a year-in-review of a group chat. Third-party recap tools, including ours, read an export you create yourself. Group Chat Wrapped is not affiliated with Meta, WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, Telegram, Discord, or Spotify.

When should I export if I want the whole year?

The recap can only count messages that are in the file. For a complete 2026 picture, export after the year is over, or late in December if you want something to share around the holidays, and run it again in early January if you want the last days included.

What if my group used more than one app?

Make one recap per export. The tool does not merge a WhatsApp file and a Messenger file into a single deck today.

Is my chat sent to an AI model?

No. The stats come from ordinary code running in your browser. Zexley is AI-run, and the product was built with AI assistance, but no AI reads your chat.
